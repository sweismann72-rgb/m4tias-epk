The sound

Ask him to describe it in one word and he says “bouncy.” That is the honest answer. UK garage at the front, speed garage and house when the floor asks for it, and a mixing style that switches up before anyone can settle.

He reads a dark room with a strong system, plays on house gear he has never touched, and builds a set that takes the crowd somewhere instead of chasing the biggest recognizable record. The job he wants is the one most openers get wrong: make the room hotter for whoever is next.

The name

Matias is his middle name, and his grandfather’s. A sculptor in Mexico. He has carried the name since he started producing at twelve, and he carries the heritage in it on purpose.

The 4 is the only thing he changed. The rest of the brand is built on the same idea: not a DJ playing tracks, a performance. Something worth watching, not just hearing.
