The sound

Bouncy, fast, and never where you left it

UK garage at the front. Speed garage and house when the floor asks for it. A mixing style that switches up before anyone can settle, and a reputation for being the DJ people watch, not just hear.

M4TIAS reads a dark room with a strong system, plays on house gear he has never touched, and builds a set that takes the crowd somewhere instead of chasing the biggest recognizable record. He treats the opening and direct-support slots the way they should be treated: make the room hotter for whoever is next.

About

M4TIAS

M4TIAS is Sean Danahy, a DJ and producer out of Denver. UK garage, speed garage, and house, built to move a room from the first record.

One week this May: Shelter in Encinitas on the Thursday, nine until close, then headlining the San Diego Takeover on the Saturday with Orly Gal, KOA, and MVRCI. Back home, a first-place finish at The Black Box’s DJ competition put him through to the Electronic Tuesdays championship final with a residency on the line.

Matias is his middle name and his grandfather’s, a sculptor in Mexico. He has carried it since he started producing at twelve. The 4 is the only thing he changed.
