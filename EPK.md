An intimate, high-energy club night centered around UK garage, bass, house, and underground electronic music. A dark room with a strong sound system, a packed dance floor, and a crowd that is open-minded, energetic, and there to discover music. Direct support or a late-night slot once the room has filled and the energy is building, so he can play faster and more energetic while still taking the crowd somewhere and setting up the next artist.
